Snap Linkit Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026 | Version 1.5.0

The Short Version

Snap Linkit does not collect, send, or share any information about you. It only keeps your settings, inside your own browser.

Zero Data Collection Nothing Sent to Us No Tracking Open Source

How We Verified This

We don't just claim privacy—we prove it. Every line of code has been analyzed via static code analysis:

  • No fetch() - The function that makes HTTP requests doesn't exist in our code
  • No XMLHttpRequest - The older method for network calls isn't used
  • No WebSocket - No real-time connections to any server
  • No external URLs - No hardcoded endpoints, APIs, or tracking pixels
  • No analytics libraries - No Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, or any tracking SDK

You can verify this yourself: Open your browser's Developer Tools (F12), go to the Network tab, and use the extension. Snap Linkit sends nothing on its own; the only requests you will see are the pages and files you choose to open or download.

Data Collection

We collect NOTHING. Specifically:

  • No personal information
  • No browsing history
  • No usage analytics
  • No cookies or tracking
  • No crash reports
  • No telemetry of any kind

Network Requests

Snap Linkit never sends anything to us or to anyone else. It has no server and contacts none. The only network traffic it causes is what you ask for: when you choose to open or download the links you selected, Firefox loads them just as if you had clicked them yourself. When you tick Fandom progress tracking checkboxes, the Fandom page itself saves each change to your Fandom account; Snap Linkit only clicks the box. You can verify this using your browser's developer tools (Network tab).

What Snap Linkit Stores

Snap Linkit keeps only your settings and one notice flag, using Firefox's built-in storage for add-ons. Here is the complete list:

  • scrollEdgeThreshold - How close to screen edge triggers auto-scroll (default: 50px)
  • autoScrollSpeed - Speed of auto-scroll (default: 15)
  • maxTabsWithoutConfirm - Tab count before confirmation dialog (default: 20)
  • tabOpenDelay - Milliseconds between opening tabs (default: 50ms)
  • blacklist - The sites you add under Site Filtering (default: empty)
  • blacklistMode - Blacklist or whitelist mode (default: blacklist)
  • extensionEnabled - Whether extension is on (default: true)
  • releaseAction - What happens when you let go after a drag over links (default: open in new tabs)
  • checkboxSaveGap - Wait after each save of a Fandom progress tracking checkbox (default: 150ms)
  • whatsNew - One-time "what's new" notice after an update; kept only on this device and removed when you click "Got it"

That's it: 9 settings and 1 notice flag. This data:

  • Is never sent to us, and we cannot see it
  • Is deleted from your browser when you remove Snap Linkit
  • Contains nothing about you or your browsing, apart from any site names you type into Site Filtering yourself

If you use Firefox Sync: your 9 settings are saved in Firefox's sync storage. If you are signed in to a Mozilla account with "Add-ons" ticked in Firefox's Sync settings, Firefox copies them to your other computers through Mozilla's servers. Firefox encrypts them before they leave your browser, so Mozilla cannot read them (Mozilla accounts privacy notice). Without Sync, they stay on this device. The notice flag (whatsNew) always stays on this device only.

Permissions Explained

These are all the permissions Snap Linkit asks for, and why:

Permission Why We Need It
clipboardWrite Lets "Copy Links", "Copy Link Text", the "Copy links" choice of "When I let go", and Ctrl+C put the links you selected on your clipboard.
storage Lets Snap Linkit remember your settings and the one-time notice.
downloads Lets "Download Files" save the files you selected.
http://*/*, https://*/* (access to websites) Lets Snap Linkit draw the selection box and find the links on the web pages you use it on; nothing from those pages is sent anywhere. It also lets the popup read the address of the tab you're on, so it can tell you when a Site Filtering rule turns Snap Linkit off there. Only the site name is used, inside your browser; it isn't stored or sent anywhere.

Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties because we don't have any data to share. There are no analytics services, no advertising networks, no data brokers. If you use Firefox Sync, your settings pass through Mozilla's servers in encrypted form, as explained above; that is a Firefox feature you control, not something Snap Linkit sends.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, there is no children's data to protect.

Open Source

This extension's source code is available for inspection. We have nothing to hide because we have nothing to collect. You are welcome to review the code to verify our privacy claims.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Given that we collect nothing, significant changes are unlikely.
